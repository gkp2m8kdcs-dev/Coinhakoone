Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
All portfolios, watchlists, alerts, and trade journal data are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App will erase that data.
Does Coinhakoone connect to my exchange account?
No. Coinhakoone does not connect to exchanges, custody funds, or execute trades. It is a tracking and analytics tool only.
Why are prices delayed or different from my exchange?
Market data is sourced from third-party providers and may be delayed by seconds to minutes. Aggregated prices may differ from a specific exchange order book.
Can I export my data?
Export options are available within the App for trades and portfolio snapshots. Look for the export/share entry under Settings.
How do I reset the App?
Go to Settings → Advanced → Reset All Data. This action is irreversible and clears all locally stored data.
Do you provide financial advice?
No. Coinhakoone is an informational tool. Nothing in the App constitutes financial, investment, or trading advice. Always do your own research.
