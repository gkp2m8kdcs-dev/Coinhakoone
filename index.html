About Coinhakoone

Coinhakoone is a powerful mobile companion for cryptocurrency enthusiasts, traders, and long-term investors. It combines real-time market data, portfolio tracking, and advanced analytics into a single, beautifully designed iOS application.

Whether you are checking live prices, building a diversified portfolio, simulating dollar-cost averaging strategies, or comparing assets on the go, Coinhakoone keeps you oriented in fast-moving markets.

Key Features

Live Markets
Real-time prices, market caps, and 24h changes across thousands of coins.
Portfolio Tracking
Track holdings, cost basis, and realized/unrealized P&L with clarity.
DCA Simulator
Model dollar-cost averaging strategies across historical periods.
Converter & Compare
Convert between any crypto and fiat currencies; compare assets side by side.
Watchlist & Alerts
Keep an eye on coins and get notified on price and indicator triggers.
Heatmap & Insights
Visualize market momentum and discover trending narratives.
Trade Journal
Log trades with receipts and analyze execution over time.
Rebalancer
Target allocations and generate rebalancing plans for your portfolio.

Designed for Clarity

Coinhakoone is crafted with a dark-first interface, smooth animations, and intuitive gestures. Every screen is designed to help you make better decisions, faster — without the noise.

What you should know

  • Coinhakoone is an informational and tracking tool only. It does not execute trades, hold custody of funds, or provide financial advice.
  • All market data is sourced from third-party providers and may be delayed or inaccurate.
  • Past performance is not indicative of future results. Always do your own research.
