Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Coinhakoone ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Coinhakoone (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account & Profile Data: Information you enter when setting up watchlists, portfolios, alerts, and trade logs.
  • Receipt Images: Photos you choose to capture or attach to trade entries. These remain stored locally on your device unless explicitly synced.
  • Support Communications: When you contact us, we collect the content of your message and your contact details.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, locale, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated usage events to help us improve features and stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App's features.
  • Persist your portfolios, watchlists, alerts, and trade journals across sessions.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues.
  • Monitor usage trends and diagnose technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Permissions

Coinhakoone requests only the permissions it needs:

  • Camera: To optionally capture receipt photos for trade entries.
  • Photo Library: To attach existing receipt images to trade entries.

These permissions are entirely optional. The App remains fully usable without granting them.

5. Third-Party Services

Coinhakoone uses third-party services to deliver market data and analytics. These providers may receive anonymous identifiers or technical data necessary to deliver their services:

  • Market Data Providers: To retrieve cryptocurrency prices, charts, and metadata.
  • Crash & Analytics Services: To collect anonymized diagnostics.

Coinhakoone does not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your portfolio, watchlist, alerts, and trade journal data are stored locally on your device. We employ reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Coinhakoone is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Because most data is stored locally, you can clear it by uninstalling the App or by using in-app reset options.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the App and on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
